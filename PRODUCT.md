# Product

Name: Bible Study

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

One person: the owner, studying Scripture for personal use. They work with English, Hebrew and Greek texts side by side and move between searching, reading, word analysis and note-taking in one sitting. This is a personal, non-commercial tool, not a distributed product.

## Product Purpose

A modern Rust reimplementation of BibleWorks 8. It keeps the original's research workflow (search, browse, analyze, annotate) and replaces its 2009 Windows UI with a fast, calm, usable interface. It reads the owner's own installed BibleWorks 8 modules from disk at runtime; it never copies or redistributes them. Success means the owner can do a day of study in it without opening BibleWorks 8.

Scope, in order: search and verse browser; word analysis (lexicon, parsing); notes, verse lists and word lists; then maps, timeline, diagrams and flashcards.

## Positioning

BibleWorks' research depth with the speed and clarity of a modern tool.

## Brand Personality

Calm by default, dense on demand. Reading space is quiet and typographic; power-user panes (parallel versions, search results, analysis) can be opened or collapsed, and a command palette drives search.

## Anti-references

None stated yet.

## Design Principles

- The text is the interface: scripture and results get the space and the best typography; chrome recedes.
- Calm default, dense on demand: every pane can collapse or expand without losing state.
- Keyboard first: search, navigation and panes all work from a command palette and shortcuts.
- Familiar over novel: standard affordances, because the owner is fluent in research software.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Hebrew (right-to-left) and Greek must render correctly with proper fonts and vowel points. Beyond that, no specific requirements stated yet.
